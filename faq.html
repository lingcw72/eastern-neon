Tender & project FAQ

Ready to support commercial signage work.

Practical answers for property owners, developers, consultants and procurement teams considering Eastern Neon for a tender or direct project.

Can Eastern Neon be invited to tender directly?

Yes. Send the tender invitation, scope, drawings, programme and submission requirements directly to EN. We will review the information needed to determine the appropriate proposal route.

What should a tender invitation include?

Provide the sign locations, drawings or elevations, client-supplied artwork and brand guidelines, required scope, access restrictions, programme, site contact and tender return requirements. Existing-sign photographs and dimensions are helpful for replacement or maintenance works.

Does EN create logos or graphic design?

No. EN works from the client's approved logo artwork, brand guidelines and technical requirements, then develops the signage for fabrication, installation, repair or maintenance.

What types of work does EN undertake?

EN supports commercial and institutional building signage, including façade and rooftop signs, replacements, refurbishment, inspection, repair, maintenance and high-access installation.

Does EN still work with traditional neon?

Yes. Traditional neon fabrication, repair and restoration is a core EN capability. EN is one of the last traditional neon specialists in Singapore, supporting signs where this craft and its technical requirements remain essential.

Can EN assist with access, engineering and authority requirements?

EN can coordinate the relevant technical documents, access planning and submission support appropriate to the project. The exact requirements depend on the sign, building and proposed works.

What tender and safety documentation is available?

Depending on the procurement requirements, EN can provide relevant company, safety, insurance and project documentation. Confirm the specific document list and validity requirements in the tender invitation.

Can EN provide ongoing maintenance after installation?

Yes. EN can discuss inspection, repair and planned maintenance requirements for installed or existing signage, including elevated and illuminated signs.
