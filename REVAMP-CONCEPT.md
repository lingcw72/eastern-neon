# Eastern Neon Website Revamp — Concept Notes

Status: Working discussion document for later review. These ideas are not final website copy or approved requirements.

## 1. Purpose of the revamp

The revamped website should position Eastern Neon as a specialist in large-scale, technically demanding building signage in Singapore.

It should not present the company as a general neon-sign vendor or a supplier of decorative lighting for small consumer and retail projects.

The website has two principal jobs:

1. Establish credibility when a prospect checks Eastern Neon after being contacted.
2. Generate and support enquiries from building owners and professionals who need help with new, faulty, ageing or poorly maintained façade signage.

## 2. Commercial positioning

### Core focus

Eastern Neon is primarily a B2B company focused on substantial building-level signage, including:

- Rooftop and high-rise building signage
- Large exterior façade signs
- Corporate headquarters and commercial-property signage
- Shopping malls, hotels, hospitals, industrial buildings and major institutions
- Signage requiring structural planning and professional installation
- Projects involving rope access or other high-access methods
- Professional Engineer endorsement and BCA submission support
- Repair, refurbishment and ongoing maintenance of major signage

### Work the company does not wish to target

- Decorative neon for restaurants, bars, karaoke venues or homes
- Small shopfront signs
- Interior ambience lighting
- One-off consumer purchases
- Small retail-renovation packages

This distinction should be evident in the imagery, language, navigation, enquiry form and project selection.

### Working positioning statement

> Large-scale building signage, engineered and installed for Singapore's skyline.

### Alternative service-led statement

> We help building owners keep prominent signage safe, illuminated and looking its best—from inspection and repair to replacement, regulatory support and long-term maintenance.

### Target audiences

- Building and property owners
- Property developers
- Architects and design consultants
- Main contractors
- Facilities managers
- Managing agents and MCSTs
- Corporate real-estate teams
- Procurement teams
- Hotel, hospital, mall and institutional operators

## 3. How business is currently generated

Traditional advertising channels such as printed T-shirts and advertising on taxis, buses or cars do not appear to be effective sources of business.

A meaningful lead-generation method is proactive observation:

1. The Eastern Neon team notices faulty or poorly maintained façade signage while travelling around Singapore at night.
2. The company identifies and contacts the relevant business, building owner, facilities team or managing agent.
3. The prospect visits the website to assess Eastern Neon's credibility.
4. Eastern Neon inspects the signage and proposes repair, refurbishment, replacement or maintenance.
5. Eastern Neon may then become an approved vendor.
6. The vendor relationship can lead to recurring maintenance and future signage work.

### Implication for the website

The website is not only an advertising channel. It is an important vendor-validation and trust-building tool.

After receiving a cold call, a prospect should quickly find evidence of:

- Experience with large-scale and high-rise signage
- Recognisable completed projects
- Safe work-at-height and rope-access capability
- Inspection, diagnosis, repair, replacement and maintenance capabilities
- PE endorsement and BCA submission support
- Professional documentation and procurement readiness
- Long-term maintenance capability
- Clear company identity and contact details

### Potential formal service

**Building Signage Inspection & Maintenance** could be presented as a clear service for facilities and property teams.

The service should focus on early detection, safety, reliability, appearance and brand visibility—not fear-based selling.

## 4. Role of the website in the sales journey

The website should support two high-value moments:

### Prospect validation

A property or facilities professional has been contacted by Eastern Neon and visits the site to determine whether the company is experienced, credible and suitable for vendor registration.

### Active problem-solving

A building manager searches for help with faulty, ageing or large-scale façade signage and discovers Eastern Neon.

### Trust signals to prioritise

- Large completed-project photography
- Named projects and clients where permission exists
- Clear scope of work on project pages
- Years of relevant experience, once verified
- Safety qualifications and accreditations, once verified
- Engineering and regulatory capabilities
- High-access installation experience
- Maintenance capability
- Contact details and business address
- Procurement and vendor-registration information where appropriate

## 5. Proposed process page

### Working page title

**From Concept to Skyline**

### Purpose

The page should give clients confidence that Eastern Neon understands and manages the complete process. It should demonstrate competence without overwhelming the reader or disclosing proprietary methods.

Rather than displaying twelve equally weighted steps, the process can be grouped into five phases.

### Phase 1 — Understand the site

**Briefing and site survey**

Eastern Neon begins by understanding the client's brand, building, visibility objectives, technical constraints and approval requirements. The team surveys the façade, mounting conditions, electrical supply, access routes, viewing angles and existing signage.

Potential deliverables:

- Site measurements and photographs
- Access assessment
- Visibility considerations
- Initial project requirements

### Phase 2 — Design the solution

**Concept, engineering and costing**

The brand is translated into a façade-signage concept appropriate for the building. The proposal considers scale, materials, illumination, mounting, environmental exposure, maintenance access and day/night appearance.

Potential deliverables:

- Signage concepts
- Building visualisations
- Material and lighting specifications
- Preliminary fixing strategy
- Project quotation and programme

### Phase 3 — Secure approvals

**Owner consent, PE endorsement and authority submissions**

Eastern Neon coordinates technical drawings and documents needed for building-owner approval and regulatory submission. Where required, a Professional Engineer reviews and endorses the structure, and applications are submitted for the applicable BCA licence and Permit To Use.

Potential deliverables:

- Elevation and sectional drawings
- Fixing and structural details
- Owner or MCST submission documents
- PE-endorsed drawings where required
- BCA application support

All regulatory claims should say “where required” because the approval route depends on the sign's size, support structure, height, location and property type.

### Phase 4 — Build and install

**Fabrication, testing and high-access installation**

The approved sign is fabricated and tested before installation. Eastern Neon plans the lifting, access, electrical, work-at-height, pedestrian-control and building-protection requirements before site work begins.

Potential deliverables:

- Fabricated sign and support structure
- Factory illumination testing
- Installation method statement
- Risk assessment and safe-work procedures
- On-site installation and electrical connection

The public website should mention that these controlled documents exist without publishing their contents.

### Phase 5 — Protect the investment

**Commissioning, handover and maintenance**

After installation, the sign is inspected and tested during the day and at night. The client receives the relevant handover documents, warranty information and recommended maintenance plan.

Potential deliverables:

- Completed installation checks
- Day and night testing
- As-built information
- Warranty documentation
- Preventive-maintenance options
- Repair and licence-renewal support

### Working closing message

> One partner from survey to long-term maintenance.
>
> Large-scale façade signage involves architecture, engineering, regulation, fabrication, access, installation and ongoing care. Eastern Neon coordinates every stage so building owners and project teams have one experienced partner accountable for the complete result.

### Suggested call to action

**Discuss a building signage project**

Potential enquiry fields:

- Building name and address
- New sign, replacement or repair
- Approximate installation height
- Building-owner or MCST approval status
- Desired completion date
- Drawings or photographs
- Contact information

### Recommended process-page photography

- On-site surveying
- Technical drawings or design review
- Fabrication
- High-access or rope-access installation
- Day and night testing
- Completed façade signage

## 6. Educational content and trade-secret boundaries

Educational content can help clients, search engines and AI systems understand Eastern Neon's expertise. It should demonstrate knowledge without becoming a technical manual for competitors.

### General principle

> Explain what happens, why it matters and what the client receives—but not proprietary calculations, methods, suppliers, costs or internal decision rules.

### Suitable for public explanation

- Broad project phases
- Roles and responsibilities
- Client inputs
- Typical deliverables
- Reasons approvals and engineering matter
- Common causes of delay
- General safety and maintenance principles
- Public regulatory requirements
- Completed outcomes and case studies

### Keep confidential

- Structural calculations and engineering assumptions
- Detailed fixing and anchoring methods
- Bracket and support-frame designs
- Material thicknesses and proprietary specifications
- Electrical layouts and component configurations
- Supplier and subcontractor identities
- Fabrication techniques
- Equipment-selection criteria
- Labour estimates, costing formulas, margins and pricing
- Internal inspection thresholds and diagnostic methods
- Method statements, risk assessments and safe-work procedures
- Tender strategy and authority-submission templates
- Building access and security details

### Three-level content policy

| Level | Treatment | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public knowledge | Explain clearly | Signage categories, broad project phases and licence basics |
| Expertise indicators | Describe the outcome, not the method | “Engineered for the site's structural and environmental conditions” |
| Proprietary knowledge | Keep confidential | Calculations, fixing systems, specifications, work methods and costs |

### Appropriate technical language

Avoid exposing specifications such as anchor types, spacing and material thicknesses.

Prefer language such as:

> Each support system is designed for the building substrate, sign dimensions, environmental exposure and maintenance requirements, with PE review where required.

Instead of publishing an installation procedure, say:

> Before installation, we prepare the required access, lifting, electrical and safety plans for the site. These are reviewed with the relevant project stakeholders before work begins.

### Potential educational topics

- What approvals are required for building signage in Singapore?
- When is a Professional Engineer needed?
- What is the difference between a signboard and an advertising sign?
- How long does a façade-signage project take?
- Who is responsible for building-owner consent?
- How is high-rise signage maintained?
- What should a facilities manager do when part of a sign stops illuminating?
- When should an old façade sign be repaired rather than replaced?

Each page should answer the client's decision-level question and stop before disclosing execution-level detail.

A suitable closing qualification is:

> Requirements differ according to the sign's size, support structure, height, location and building ownership. Eastern Neon can assess the site and advise on the appropriate project and approval route.

## 7. Project and portfolio experience

### Purpose

The portfolio should demonstrate the scale, complexity and quality of Eastern Neon's work. It should feel like an architectural and engineering portfolio, not a catalogue of small signage products.

### Portfolio thumbnails

Use a consistent square display crop for portfolio cards.

The source photography should remain high-resolution and uncropped. The website should create square thumbnails while allowing project pages to use landscape and portrait images where these show the building more effectively.

### Recommended grid

- Desktop: 3 columns × 4 rows
- Tablet: 2 columns × 6 rows
- Mobile: 1 column × 12 rows
- Initial display: 12 projects

Three desktop columns are preferred to four because large façade projects need visual presence and architectural context. A four-column grid may make projects resemble small catalogue products.

After the first twelve projects, use either:

- **Load more projects**, or
- Simple category filtering with pagination

Avoid loading the entire historical portfolio on one page.

### Information on each project card

- Project category
- Project or building name
- Location, where useful
- Completion year, once confirmed
- One-line scope of work

Example:

> High-rise façade signage  
> One Raffles Place  
> Fabrication and high-access installation of illuminated building signage.

### Suggested categories

Categories should reflect buyer needs rather than only signage materials:

- High-rise and rooftop signage
- Building façade signage
- Corporate and industrial
- Hotels and hospitality
- Retail and commercial developments
- Institutional and public-sector
- Repair and refurbishment

A project can have multiple internal tags, but the visible interface should remain simple.

### Project-detail pages

Each project page should use approximately six to ten strong photographs. Fewer excellent photographs are preferable to many repetitive images.

Suggested sequence:

1. Finished building at night
2. Finished building during the day
3. Wider streetscape context
4. Closer signage detail
5. Installation or rope-access photograph
6. Fabrication or technical-detail photograph
7. Additional completed angle where useful

Potential project information:

- Client or building name
- Location
- Project category
- Completion date
- Client challenge
- Eastern Neon's scope
- Constraints or complexity, described without proprietary detail
- Completed outcome
- Services involved

## 8. Photography brief — initial direction

The photographer should be asked to:

- Deliver high-resolution original files
- Leave sufficient space around the sign for multiple crops
- Compose important subjects so square crops remain possible
- Capture both day and illuminated night views
- Include wide building context
- Include closer signage details
- Record safe, approved installation activity where possible
- Avoid revealing confidential drawings, access information or security details

Square should be a standard website crop, not necessarily the photographer's only shooting format.
