### Draft company summary

Eastern Neon Pte Ltd is a Singapore signage company that provides design consultation, graphic design, production, installation, repair and maintenance. Its stated capabilities include neon, cold cathode, LED, acrylic and digital signage; professional rope access; Professional Engineer endorsements; and BCA submissions.

## Services

### Signage design and customisation

- Signage consultation and design
- Graphic design
- Custom signage production
- Delivery and installation

### LED signage

The current website describes Eastern Neon as providing consultation, design, production, delivery, installation, servicing and maintenance for LED signs. Claimed benefits include brightness, wide viewing angles, low operating costs and minimal maintenance.

### Neon and cold cathode signage

The company states that it designs, installs, services and restores neon signage, ranging from window signs to high-rise corporate signs. The current page says Eastern Neon has handled neon-signage requirements for “over 20 years”; this should be replaced with a specific, verified history before launch.

### Acrylic signage

The company states that it produces, prints and installs custom acrylic signage for indoor and outdoor use, including offices and lobbies. Available options are described as including different colours, sizes, thicknesses, finishes, dimensional lettering, logos and stand-off mounting.

### Digital signage and screens

Digital signage and screens appear in the About page’s service list, but the current website provides no dedicated description. Confirm the products, technology, installation scope and maintenance support offered.

### Installation

Installation is listed as a core specialisation. The current website refers to both conventional installation teams and access solutions for difficult or elevated locations.

### Repairs, refurbishment and maintenance

- Ad-hoc signage repairs
- Sign restoration and refurbishment
- Maintenance contracts
- LED-sign servicing and maintenance
- Work on both existing and newly installed signage

The existing navigation includes a separate **Refurbish Signage** page, but its text was not available during this inventory.

### Professional rope access

The company states that it offers professional rope-access services using qualified abseilers and elevated work-platform operators, particularly where accessibility and safety are important. Confirm staff certifications, governing standards and the exact scope of work before making stronger safety claims.

### Professional Engineer endorsements and BCA submissions

Eastern Neon states that it helps clients obtain Professional Engineer (PE) endorsements and submit applications for Temporary Building Permits. Its signage proposals are described as being checked for design, fixture safety and feasibility.

Confirm whether the company handles:

- New Temporary Building Permit applications
- Permit renewals
- PE structural endorsements
- BCA submissions from start to approval
- Other authority submissions or licences
